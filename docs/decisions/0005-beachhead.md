# 0005 — Beachhead: German cities first, Europe as the goal

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-17
- **Amended:** 2026-08-03 — clarified that Europe is the destination, not a later idea.

## Context
Density beats spread for a two-sided marketplace: a club only benefits if other clubs, fighters and spectators are already there.

## Decision
Start in German cities — Berlin, Hamburg, Cologne, Munich — and drive **one** to density before opening the second. Europe is the eventual market, and the product is built multi-region and multi-language from day one.

## Consequences
- Ingestion, currency, language and date handling are Europe-shaped from the start; no German hardcoding.
- Conversations from outside the starting cities are logged and marked out-of-beachhead. They inform sequencing; they are not permission to widen early.
- "Which city is at density" is a reviewable question at every weekly steering session.
