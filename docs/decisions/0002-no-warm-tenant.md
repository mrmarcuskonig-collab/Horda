# 0002 — No warm tenant; build supply cold

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-17
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context
Launching a two-sided sports-events product is far easier with a warm club or fighter relationship. There is none.

## Decision
Accept that supply is built from cold outreach, and make Tenant Acquisition the top external priority.

## Consequences
- The evidence loop (`docs/evidence/`) is the sales pipeline, not a research exercise.
- Cold-outreach conversion is the single most important unknown in the plan; measure it from conversation one.
- Product work is subordinate to acquisition until the first tenants pay.
